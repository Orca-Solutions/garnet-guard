# 0010. Go/no-go gates for the hosted app

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

Impact comes before income, and Jason builds solo. Building a hosted product nobody pays for would cost the open-source tool time. plans/monetization.md proposed two gates.

## Decision

- **Gate A (month 4):** build a hosted app only if there are at least 15 interest-list orgs, at least 5 with 20+ repos, and at least 2 verbally agree to a paid pilot.
- **Gate B (end of month 6):** continue only if at least 2 orgs have paid and at least 1 would renew at list price. Otherwise stop hosted work; Garnet continues on grants and sponsors.
- Until Gate B passes, money work stays under about 20% of Jason's time and pauses first if open-source quality slips.

## Consequences

Garnet may never have a paid product, and that is an acceptable outcome. The pricing shape is in ADR-0012.

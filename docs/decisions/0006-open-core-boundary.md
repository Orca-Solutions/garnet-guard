# 0006. Open core boundary

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

Jason wants impact first, with income where it doesn't compromise impact or learning. Socket and Aikido give away a wrapper and sell org policy. Garnet can go further by keeping the whole engine open.

## Decision

All detection, surfaces and local policy are free under Apache-2.0. A separate, paid, hosted GitHub App sells org-wide policy, cross-repo trust history, alerting, audit and SSO. The commitments are in OPEN_CORE.md. No paid work starts before month 4, and pricing is validated with paying design partners.

## Consequences

Revenue depends on the coordination and history features being worth paying for. The open-source project must never depend on the hosted service.
